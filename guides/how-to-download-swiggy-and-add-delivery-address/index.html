Download Swiggy, sign up and add your delivery address

4:33 min
Written by Caroline D'Cruz, journalist and editor with 16+ years of experience, founder of The Elders Club · Last updated 22 October 2026

Swiggy brings food to your door, but first it needs a few things from you. This guide walks through installing Swiggy from the Play Store, signing up with your mobile number, understanding the location and notification pop-ups, and saving your delivery address, one screen at a time.

The steps, written out

1

Open the Play Store

On your phone, open the Play Store.

On screen: The Play Store icon

2

Tap the search bar

Tap the search bar at the top.

On screen: The search bar

3

Type Swiggy

Type Swiggy.

On screen: Typing Swiggy in the search bar

4

Tap Install

Tap Install next to Swiggy.

On screen: The Install button

5

Tap Open

When the button says Open, tap Open.

On screen: The Open button

6

Type your mobile number

Swiggy opens. Type your mobile number.

On screen: The mobile number box

7

Type the 6-digit code

You'll get a 6-digit code by SMS. Type it in.

On screen: The 6-digit code boxes

8

Type your name

Type your name when Swiggy asks for it.

On screen: The name box

9

Allow location

Swiggy will ask for your location. Tap While using the app.

On screen: The location pop-up

10

Turn on location

If your phone's location is off, Swiggy will ask you to turn it on. This helps Swiggy find restaurants near you and deliver to the right place. Tap Turn on.

On screen: The Turn on button

11

Turn on notifications

Swiggy will ask about notifications. They tell you when your order is confirmed, on its way, or has arrived. Tap Turn on Notification.

On screen: The Turn on Notification button

12

Tap Allow

Then tap Allow.

On screen: The Allow button

13

Tap your address at the top

Tap the address at the top of the screen.

On screen: The address bar at the top

14

Tap Add New Address

Tap Add New Address.

On screen: The Add New Address button

15

Place the pin and confirm

A map opens. Place the pin at your exact location, then tap Confirm and proceed.

16

Add your flat and building

Fill in your flat or house number, your building or street name, and check that the area is right.

On screen: The address details form

17

Choose House, Office or Other

Choose House, Office or Other. This label helps you tell your addresses apart. You can save more than one address, like home, office or a relative's, and choose the right one when you order.

Tap Save Address

Tap Save Address.

On screen: The Save Address button

20

See your address at the top

Your address now shows at the top of the screen. You're ready to order.

On screen: The address at the top of the home screen

If something goes wrong

Why does Swiggy ask for my location?

So it can find restaurants near you and deliver your food to the right place. Tap While using the app.

Why does Swiggy ask to send notifications?

They tell you when your order is confirmed, on its way and arrived. You can tap Allow.

Can I save more than one address?

Yes. Save each one as House, Office or Other, for example your home, your office or a relative's, and choose the right one when you order.

Do I have to give my name and number?

Yes. Swiggy uses your mobile number and the 6-digit code it sends by SMS to sign you up.

What should I check before I order?

Always check your delivery address. And never share your OTP or PIN with anyone who calls you.

What you'll walk away knowing

By the end of this guide, made free and step-by-step by The Elders Club, you'll be able to:

  • Download and install Swiggy
  • Sign up with your mobile number and the SMS code
  • Understand the location and notification pop-ups
  • Add, label and save your delivery address

Once your address is saved and showing at the top of the app, you're ready to order. Always check that address before you place an order, and never share your OTP with anyone.

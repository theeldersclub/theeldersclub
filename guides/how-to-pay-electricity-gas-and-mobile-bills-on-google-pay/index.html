Pay electricity, gas & phone bills on Google Pay

3:16 min
Written by Caroline D'Cruz, journalist and editor with 16+ years of experience, founder of The Elders Club · Last updated 9 September 2026

Paying an electricity, gas, or mobile bill on Google Pay takes the same few steps every time, once you've done it once. This guide walks through three real bills side by side, so you can see the pattern for yourself and pay any bill with confidence. None of it needs a branch visit or a phone call — every payment happens from the same screen, in the same few taps.

The steps, written out

1

Open Bills & recharges

From the Google Pay home screen, tap "Bills & recharges." This is where every biller — electricity, gas, mobile, and more — lives in one place.

On screen: Bills & recharges section

2

Choose your electricity board

Search for or select your electricity board from the list. If you've paid before, it will already be saved here — no need to search again.

On screen: Choose your electricity board

3

Check the amount, then tap Pay

Your current bill amount appears on screen. Read it carefully — check the amount matches what you expect — then tap Pay.

On screen: Check the amount, then tap Pay

4

Book and pay for a gas cylinder

Under Bills & recharges, choose Gas cylinder booking and link the mobile number registered with your gas provider. Google Pay finds your account using that number.

On screen: Gas cylinder booking screen

5

Pay a postpaid mobile bill

Choose your mobile provider under Bills & recharges, check the amount shown, and confirm payment the same way as any other bill.

On screen: Postpaid mobile bill screen

6

Enter your UPI PIN to finish

Every bill payment ends with your UPI PIN, the same as sending money to a person. The moment you enter it, the payment goes through.

On screen: Entering the UPI PIN

7

Know what success looks like

A green confirmation banner appears the moment a payment succeeds, and the bill is marked Paid. That's your sign it's genuinely gone through — no need to check anywhere else.

On screen: Payment success confirmation

8

Keep your PIN and OTP private

Just like sending money, your UPI PIN and any OTP are yours alone. No biller, bank, or Google Pay will ever ask you for either one over a call or message.

On screen: Reminder: never share your PIN or OTP

If something goes wrong

What if I don't know which electricity board to choose?

Type the name of your city, state electricity board, or the name printed on your paper bill into the search bar — Google Pay will show matching boards. Once you pay once, it's saved for next time, so you won't need to search again.

Can I pay my gas bill before the cylinder is delivered?

Gas cylinder bookings are usually paid at the time of booking, not after delivery — linking your registered mobile number lets Google Pay find your existing booking and take payment for it directly.

What if the amount shown looks wrong?

Don't pay it. Close the screen and check your paper bill or the biller's own app or website first. If it still looks wrong, contact the biller directly — Google Pay simply displays whatever amount the biller has sent.

How do I know a bill payment actually went through?

A green confirmation banner appears immediately, and the bill's status changes to Paid on the Bills & recharges screen. You can also check History from the home screen at any time to see every past payment.

Can I set up automatic payments so I never miss a bill?

Yes — many billers on Google Pay offer an "Autopay" option once you've paid manually the first time. It's worth turning on for bills that are the same every month, like a fixed broadband or subscription charge.

Is it safe to save my biller details for next time?

Yes. Saved billers only store which account or board you pay — never your UPI PIN, which is asked for fresh every single time you pay, exactly as with any other Google Pay payment.

What you'll walk away knowing

By the end of this guide, made free and step-by-step by The Elders Club, you'll be able to:

  • Pay an electricity bill with confidence
  • Book and pay for a gas cylinder
  • Pay a postpaid mobile bill
  • Recognise what a successful payment looks like

The same pattern carries over to almost any other biller on Google Pay, from broadband to insurance premiums — once you've paid one this way, paying the next one takes under a minute.

Save mobile data & free up phone storage on WhatsApp

2:39 min
Written by Caroline D'Cruz, journalist and editor with 16+ years of experience, founder of The Elders Club · Last updated 22 September 2026

WhatsApp is often the real reason a phone runs low on space or burns through mobile data faster than it should. This guide walks through turning on data-saving for calls, stopping photos and videos from downloading automatically, seeing exactly what's using your phone's storage, and using Disappearing Messages so storage doesn't quietly build back up — shown for both Android and iPhone.

The steps, written out

1

Turn on “Use Less Data for Calls”

On iPhone, tap your profile photo in the bottom right of WhatsApp, scroll down to Storage & Data, and turn on “Use Less Data for Calls” — this cuts how much mobile data your voice and video calls use, without changing how they sound or look.

On screen: iPhone Storage & Data screen

2

Stop photos, videos and audio from downloading automatically

On the same Storage & Data screen, scroll down to Media Auto-Download and uncheck Photos, Audio and Video. Every photo or video someone sends still arrives in the chat — it just won't save itself to your phone until you actually tap to open it.

On screen: Media Auto-Download settings

3

On Android, open Storage & Data the same way

Tap the three dots in the top right corner of WhatsApp, tap Settings, then scroll down to Storage & Data. You'll find the same auto-download options here — uncheck Photos, Audio, Video and Documents.

On screen: Android Settings, Storage & Data

4

See how much space WhatsApp is using

From Storage & Data, tap Manage Storage to see exactly how much room WhatsApp is taking up on your phone — useful for spotting which chats are using the most space.

On screen: Manage Storage screen

5

Delete anything you don't need

Inside Manage Storage, long-press on any photo, video, audio or message, then tap Delete. This only removes the downloaded copy from your phone — it doesn't delete it from the chat or affect the other person.

On screen: Long-press to delete a file

6

Turn on Disappearing Messages so storage doesn't build up again

In any chat or group, open Disappearing Messages and choose a timer — 24 hours, 7 days, or 90 days. Messages older than that timer clear themselves automatically, so storage doesn't quietly fill back up over time.

On screen: Setting a Disappearing Messages timer

7

Keep anything important before it disappears

Long-press on a photo, video, audio or message and tap the bookmark or save icon to keep it permanently, even in a chat with Disappearing Messages turned on. Do this for anything you'd be upset to lose.

On screen: Saving a message before it disappears

If something goes wrong

If I turn on Disappearing Messages, will I lose photos or messages I wanted to keep?

Only if you don't save them first. Anything you bookmark or save before the timer runs out stays in your chat permanently — everything else clears automatically once the timer ends.

Does turning off auto-download delete anything I've already saved?

No. It only stops new photos, videos and audio from downloading automatically going forward. Anything already saved to your phone stays exactly where it is.

Will “Use Less Data for Calls” make my calls sound or look worse?

No — it's designed to reduce data use without a noticeable drop in call quality. If a call ever sounds unclear, you can turn it back off in the same Storage & Data screen.

Is it safe to delete things from Manage Storage?

Yes — deleting a photo or video from Manage Storage only removes the copy saved on your phone. It doesn't delete the message from the chat itself or remove it for the person who sent it.

What you'll walk away knowing

By the end of this guide, made free and step-by-step by The Elders Club, you'll be able to:

  • Save mobile data on WhatsApp calls
  • Stop photos and videos from downloading automatically
  • See what's using your phone's storage, and clear it
  • Use Disappearing Messages so storage doesn't build up again

None of this deletes your chats or your messages — it only clears the downloaded copies of photos and videos sitting on your phone, so it's safe to do any time your storage starts filling up again.

UPI ID, phone number, or QR code: which to use

2:45 min
Written by Caroline D'Cruz, journalist and editor with 16+ years of experience, founder of The Elders Club · Last updated 5 September 2026

Google Pay gives you three different ways to send money — by phone number, UPI ID, or QR code — and it isn't always obvious which to use. This guide breaks down the real differences between them, and shows you how to create your own UPI ID so others can pay you too.

The steps, written out

1

Paying by phone number

Type the receiver's phone number and Google Pay finds their name automatically. It's the quickest option when you already have someone saved as a contact — but it only works if their number is linked to a bank account.

On screen: Paying a contact by phone number

2

Paying by UPI ID

A UPI ID looks like a name followed by @ and a bank, such as ravi@okhdfc. It's more precise than a phone number, works even if a phone number isn't linked to UPI, and is the safest way to pay someone who isn't already in your contacts.

On screen: Paying by typing a UPI ID

3

Paying by QR code

At a shop, or when a family member holds up their phone, scanning their QR code is usually the fastest and safest option — the recipient and often the amount are filled in for you, so there's less chance of a mistake.

On screen: Scanning a QR code to pay

4

Which one should you use

For someone already saved as a contact, phone number is fine. For anyone else, or when you want to be certain, use their UPI ID. In person, always prefer the QR code if one is available.

On screen: Comparing the three payment methods

5

Open your bank account settings

Tap your profile photo in the top right, then "Bank account," and choose the account you want to add a UPI ID to. Your bank account must already be linked before you can do this.

On screen: Bank account settings screen

6

Tap Manage UPI IDs

On the bank account screen, tap "Manage UPI IDs." This shows any UPI IDs already created for that account, such as "1/4 UPI IDs added."

On screen: Manage UPI IDs screen

7

Create your new UPI ID

Tap the Add button next to an empty slot. Google Pay creates the ID for you instantly — you don't need to type anything. You can create up to 4 UPI IDs for each bank account you add.

On screen: Creating a new UPI ID

8

Share it so people can pay you

Once created, share your UPI ID the same way you'd share a phone number — by message, or by showing your QR code, which is built from it automatically.

On screen: Sharing your UPI ID

If something goes wrong

What's the difference between paying by phone number and by UPI ID?

A phone number only works for payments if it's linked to the receiver's bank account. A UPI ID is a dedicated payment address that always works for UPI payments, whether or not a phone number is linked — which makes it more reliable, especially for someone outside your contacts.

Is a QR code payment safer than typing a phone number or UPI ID?

In person, yes — scanning a QR code avoids typos entirely, since the recipient's details (and often the amount) are already filled in. Always check the name and amount on screen before entering your UPI PIN.

How many UPI IDs can I have?

You can create up to 4 UPI IDs for each bank account you add on Google Pay. Having more than one can help if a payment fails to go through on the first try.

Do I need a UPI ID if my phone number already works for payments?

Not strictly, but it helps: a UPI ID lets people pay you even if your phone number isn't linked to UPI on their end, and it's the standard way to share your payment details in writing.

Can someone pay me if I don't have a UPI ID set up?

Yes, as long as your phone number is linked to your bank account, but setting up a UPI ID makes it more reliable and is worth doing once, upfront.

Is my UPI ID the same everywhere, or just for Google Pay?

A UPI ID works across any UPI app, not just Google Pay — so someone using PhonePe or Paytm can still pay your Google Pay UPI ID directly.

Order your first meal on Zomato

3:40 min
Written by Caroline D'Cruz, journalist and editor with 16+ years of experience, founder of The Elders Club · Last updated 10 October 2026

Ordering food on Zomato takes just a few taps once you know where to look. This guide walks through opening the app, finding a restaurant, adding food to your cart, and paying to place your order, one screen at a time. If you haven't set up Zomato yet, start with the guide on downloading it and adding your delivery address.

The steps, written out

1

Open the Zomato app

On your phone, find the red Zomato icon and tap it to open the app.

On screen: The red Zomato icon on the phone

2

See the home screen

When Zomato opens, you'll see the home screen, showing restaurants near you.

On screen: Zomato home screen

3

Tap the search bar

Tap the search bar at the top of the screen.

On screen: The search bar at the top

4

Type a restaurant or dish

Type the name of your favourite restaurant or dish.

On screen: Typing in the search bar

5

Choose your restaurant

Choose your restaurant from the results and tap on it.

On screen: Search results with a restaurant

6

Choose Veg, Egg or Non-veg

Tap Veg, Egg or Non-veg, so you only see the dishes you want to eat.

On screen: The Veg, Egg and Non-veg filters

7

Scroll to see the dishes

Scroll down to see the dishes, their prices and photos.

On screen: The restaurant's menu

8

Jump to a category

Tap Menu at the bottom of the screen, then choose a category, like Brownies, to go straight there.

On screen: The Menu button and category list

9

Add the dish to your cart

Tap ADD next to the dish you want. If it asks you to choose an option, choose one, then tap Add item.

On screen: The ADD button next to a dish

10

Tap Continue

Tap Continue at the bottom. Zomato may suggest a few more items. Tap Continue again to move ahead.

On screen: The Continue bar at the bottom

11

Check your order

Check your order. You can change the quantity here. The total is shown at the bottom, along with how you'll pay.

On screen: The cart with the total at the bottom

12

Choose how to pay

Tap Pay Using. You can pay with a UPI app, a wallet, a card or net banking, or scroll down and choose Pay on delivery.

On screen: The list of payment options

13

Place your order

Tap Place Order. Zomato places your order. If you change your mind, you can tap Cancel.

On screen: The Place Order button

If something goes wrong

How do I find a particular restaurant on Zomato?

Tap the search bar at the top of the home screen and type the name of the restaurant or dish. Then choose it from the results.

How do I see only vegetarian dishes?

On the restaurant's menu, tap Veg. You can also choose Egg or Non-veg, so you only see the dishes you want.

What does Pay on delivery mean?

It means you pay when the food arrives, instead of paying in the app. You'll find it in the list of payment options after you tap Pay Using.

Can I change my mind after tapping Place Order?

For a few moments after you tap Place Order, a Cancel button appears. Tap it if you change your mind.

What should I check before I order?

Always check the delivery address at the top of the screen. And never share your OTP or PIN with anyone who calls you.

What you'll walk away knowing

By the end of this guide, made free and step-by-step by The Elders Club, you'll be able to:

  • Find a restaurant or a dish on Zomato
  • Add food to your cart
  • Choose how you pay, including Pay on delivery
  • Place your order with confidence

Before you order, always check the delivery address at the top of the screen. And never share your OTP or PIN with anyone who calls you.

Order groceries on Blinkit

4:16 min
Written by Caroline D'Cruz, journalist and editor with 16+ years of experience, founder of The Elders Club · Last updated 18 October 2026

Blinkit brings groceries to your door in minutes, and you can order from your own phone. This guide walks through downloading the app, signing up, finding items, adding them to your cart and paying, one screen at a time.

The steps, written out

1

Open the Play Store

On your phone, open the Play Store.

On screen: The Play Store icon

2

Search for Blinkit

Tap the search bar at the top and type Blinkit.

On screen: Typing Blinkit in the search bar

3

Tap Install

Tap Install next to Blinkit.

On screen: The Install button

4

Tap Open

When the button says Open, tap Open.

On screen: The Open button

5

Allow location

Blinkit will ask for your location. This helps it find items near you and deliver to the right place. Tap While using the app.

On screen: The location pop-up

6

Sign up

Blinkit will ask for three things: your mobile number, the 6-digit code it sends by SMS, and your delivery address. Take your time and go one step at a time.

On screen: The sign-up screens

7

See the home screen

This is Blinkit's home screen. At the top you can see how fast Blinkit can deliver, and the address it will deliver to. Check the address is right before you order.

On screen: Blinkit home screen

8

Tap the search bar

Tap the search bar at the top.

On screen: The search bar at the top

9

Type what you need

Write the name of what you need, like ghee.

On screen: Typing in the search bar

10

Tap ADD

Tap ADD next to the product you want. If you want one more, tap the plus sign.

On screen: The ADD button next to a product

11

Browse by category

You can also find items by category. Scroll the top row sideways to see beauty, electronics, gifting, decor and much more.

On screen: The row of categories at the top

12

Tap View cart

Tap View cart at the bottom.

On screen: The View cart button

13

Check your cart

Check your items, the quantity and the total price before you pay.

On screen: The cart with the total

14

Choose how to pay

Choose how you want to pay. To pay with Google Pay or another UPI app, watch our Google Pay series.

On screen: The list of payment options

15

Pay on delivery

If you choose Pay on delivery, you'll pay cash when the order arrives.

On screen: The Pay on delivery option

16

Place your order

Finally, tap Place Order. Before you order, check your delivery address once more.

On screen: The Place Order button

If something goes wrong

Why does Blinkit ask for my location?

So it can find items near you and deliver to the right place. Tap While using the app.

What does Pay on delivery mean?

It means you pay in cash when your groceries arrive, instead of paying in the app.

Can I pay with Google Pay?

Yes. Choose a UPI app when you pay. If you'd like help with Google Pay, watch our Google Pay series on The Elders Club YouTube channel.

How do I find an item on Blinkit?

Tap the search bar at the top and type the name of what you need. You can also scroll the top row to browse by category.

What should I check before I place my order?

Always check your delivery address, and check your items and total in the cart. And never share your OTP or PIN with anyone who calls you.

What you'll walk away knowing

By the end of this guide, made free and step-by-step by The Elders Club, you'll be able to:

  • Download Blinkit and sign up
  • Find groceries and add them to your cart
  • Browse items by category
  • Pay, including Pay on delivery and UPI

Before you place an order, always check your delivery address. And never share your OTP or PIN with anyone who calls you.

Download Zomato, sign up and add your delivery address

3:27 min
Written by Caroline D'Cruz, journalist and editor with 16+ years of experience, founder of The Elders Club · Last updated 5 October 2026

Zomato is a popular food delivery app, and getting started takes just a few minutes. This guide walks through downloading it from the Play Store, signing in with your mobile number and OTP, and saving your delivery address, one screen at a time. It's shown on an Android phone.

The steps, written out

1

Open the Play Store

On your Android phone, open the Play Store. This is the app where you download new apps.

On screen: Play Store icon on the home screen

2

Search for Zomato

Tap Search at the bottom of the screen, then type Zomato in the search bar.

On screen: Typing Zomato in the Play Store search bar

3

Tap Install

Find Zomato: Food Delivery in the results. On the right side you'll see an Install button. Tap it, and give it a little while to install.

On screen: The Install button next to Zomato

4

Know when it's ready

When the button changes to Open, the app has finished downloading on your phone.

On screen: The Install button changed to Open

5

Open Zomato and allow location

Open the Zomato app. It will ask for location permission. Tap While using the app.

On screen: Location permission pop-up

6

Allow notifications

To get updates about your orders, tap Allow when Zomato asks about notifications.

On screen: Notifications permission pop-up

7

Enter your mobile number

Type your mobile number and tap Continue.

On screen: Mobile number sign-in screen

8

Enter the OTP

Zomato sends a 6-digit OTP to your mobile number by SMS. Type it in and your sign-in is complete.

On screen: OTP verification screen

9

Tap your location

At the top left of the Zomato home screen you'll see your location. Tap it to choose where your food should be delivered.

On screen: Location at the top of the home screen

10

Tap Add Address

On the Select a location screen, tap Add Address.

On screen: Add Address option

11

Find your area

In the search bar, type your area or road name and choose your place from the list. Then check that the pin on the map is in the right place.

On screen: Searching for an area and checking the pin

12

Label it and save

Choose Home, Work, or Other to label the address. Type your flat, floor, or house number, then tap Save address.

On screen: Choosing a label and saving the address

13

Check your address

Your address now shows at the top of the app. Before you order food, always check the address shown there.

On screen: Saved address at the top of the app

If something goes wrong

How do I know Zomato has finished downloading?

The button next to Zomato in the Play Store changes from Install to Open. That means the app is on your phone and ready to use.

What should I tap when Zomato asks for my location?

Tap While using the app. That lets Zomato use your location only while you have the app open.

Can I save more than one delivery address?

Yes. Zomato lets you save more than one address, and you can label each one Home, Work, or Other.

Should I share my OTP with anyone?

No. The OTP is only for you. Never share it with anyone, even if someone calls and says they are from Zomato.

Is this guide for iPhone or Android?

This guide is shown on an Android phone, so the screens on an iPhone may look a little different.

What you'll walk away knowing

By the end of this guide, made free and step-by-step by The Elders Club, you'll be able to:

  • Download and install Zomato from the Play Store
  • Sign in with your mobile number and OTP
  • Add and label your delivery address
  • Know what to check before you order

Once your address is saved and showing at the top of the app, you're ready to order. Always check that address before you place an order, and never share your OTP with anyone.

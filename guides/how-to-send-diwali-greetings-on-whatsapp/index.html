Send Diwali greetings on WhatsApp

4:14 min
Written by Caroline D'Cruz, journalist and editor with 16+ years of experience, founder of The Elders Club · Last updated 5 November 2026

Diwali is a lovely time to wish your family, and WhatsApp makes it easy. This guide shows three ways to send a greeting: a photo from your gallery, a moving GIF, and a greeting someone has sent you, forwarded on. Each one is explained one screen at a time.

The steps, written out

1

Open WhatsApp

Tap the WhatsApp icon on your phone's home screen.

On screen: The WhatsApp icon

2

Tap the search bar

Tap the search bar at the top of the screen.

On screen: The search bar at the top

3

Find the person

Type the name of the person you want to send a greeting to, then tap their name to open the chat.

On screen: A name typed in the search bar

4

Way 1: save a greeting to your gallery

To send a photo greeting, first save a Diwali greeting picture in your phone's Gallery.

On screen: A Diwali greeting in the Gallery

5

Tap the paperclip

Tap the paperclip icon next to the message box. On an iPhone, this is a plus sign instead.

On screen: The paperclip icon

6

Tap Gallery

Tap Gallery.

On screen: The Gallery option

7

Choose your photo

Tap the Diwali photo you like.

On screen: Choosing a photo

8

Tap Send

Tap the green Send icon. Your greeting is on its way.

On screen: The green Send icon

9

Way 2: send a GIF

A GIF is a short, moving picture. Open the chat and tap the smiley icon next to the message box.

On screen: The smiley icon

10

Tap GIF

Tap GIF.

On screen: The GIF option

11

Search for Diwali

Tap the search icon at the top, then type Diwali in the search box.

On screen: The search box with Diwali typed

12

Tap a GIF

You will see many Diwali GIFs. Tap the one you like. It sends straight away, so you don't need to tap Send.

On screen: A list of Diwali GIFs

13

Way 3: forward a greeting

If someone has sent you a Diwali greeting and you want to send it to someone else, open the chat where the greeting arrived.

On screen: A greeting in a chat

14

Tap the arrow

Tap the arrow next to the greeting.

On screen: The arrow beside the greeting

15

Choose who to send it to

Tap the person or group you want to send it to. You can choose more than one person at the same time.

On screen: Choosing people to forward to

16

Tap Send

Tap the green Send icon.

On screen: The green Send icon

17

Stay safe

At festival time, "free Diwali gift" links go around a lot. Don't tap them and don't forward them. And never share your OTP or PIN with anyone who calls or messages you.

On screen: A warning about free gift links

If something goes wrong

What is a GIF?

A GIF is a short, moving picture. WhatsApp has many of them built in, including Diwali ones, so you can search and send one in a few taps.

Where is the paperclip icon on an iPhone?

On an iPhone, the paperclip is replaced by a plus sign next to the message box. Tap it to find your photos.

Do I need to tap Send after choosing a GIF?

No. A GIF is sent as soon as you tap it.

Can I forward a greeting to more than one person?

Yes. After you tap the arrow next to a greeting, you can choose several people or groups before you tap Send.

I got a message offering a free Diwali gift. What should I do?

Don't tap the link and don't forward it. These messages return every festival season. Delete it, and never share your OTP or PIN with anyone who calls or messages you.

  • Send a moving Diwali GIF
  • Forward a greeting someone sent you
  • Spot and avoid "free Diwali gift" links

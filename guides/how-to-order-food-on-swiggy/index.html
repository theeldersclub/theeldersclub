Order food on Swiggy

3:50 min
Written by Caroline D'Cruz, journalist and editor with 16+ years of experience, founder of The Elders Club · Last updated 27 October 2026

Ordering food on Swiggy takes just a few taps once you know where to look. This guide walks through finding a restaurant, adding food to your cart, checking your order and paying, one screen at a time. If you haven't set up Swiggy yet, start with the guide on downloading it and adding your delivery address.

The steps, written out

1

Open Swiggy

After installing Swiggy from the Play Store, open it. If you haven't installed it yet, watch Part 1 of this series.

On screen: The Swiggy icon

2

See the Food screen

The Swiggy Food screen opens. Your delivery address shows at the top.

On screen: The Food screen with the address at the top

3

Scroll to see restaurants

Scroll down to see the restaurants near you.

On screen: The list of restaurants

4

Tap a restaurant

Tap the restaurant you want to order from.

On screen: A restaurant in the list

5

Add an item

Tap ADD next to the item you want. If you want one more, tap the plus sign.

On screen: The ADD button next to an item

6

Tap View Cart

Tap View Cart at the bottom of the screen.

On screen: The View Cart bar at the bottom

7

Skip Swiggy One

Sometimes Swiggy shows a membership called Swiggy One. It's an optional, paid plan with free delivery on Swiggy orders. The offer shown was Rs. 1 for 3 months, and after that you may be charged. You don't need it to order, so tap Skip for now.

On screen: The Swiggy One pop-up

8

Check your cart

Check your items, and how many of each you have chosen. Check everything before you pay.

On screen: The cart

9

Choose a delivery type

Standard is normal delivery. Eco Saver may take a little longer.

On screen: The delivery type options

10

Add delivery instructions

If you like, add instructions such as Leave at the door, or Avoid calling. You can also leave a voice note for the delivery driver.

On screen: The delivery instructions

11

Check the bill

Check the total price before you pay.

On screen: The bill

12

Choose how to pay

Choose how you want to pay.

On screen: The payment options

13

Pay on delivery

If you choose Pay on delivery, you'll pay in cash when the food arrives.

On screen: The Pay on delivery option

14

See other ways to pay

You can also pay by card, Pay Later, wallet or net banking.

On screen: More payment options

15

Pay with a UPI app

To pay with a UPI app like Google Pay, watch our Google Pay series.

On screen: The UPI payment options

16

Tap Pay

Once you've chosen how to pay, tap Pay.

On screen: The Pay button

If something goes wrong

Do I need Swiggy One to order?

No. Swiggy One is an optional, paid membership with free delivery. The offer shown was Rs. 1 for 3 months, after which you may be charged. Tap Skip for now and you can still order.

What does Pay on delivery mean?

It means you pay in cash when your food arrives, instead of paying in the app.

What is the difference between Standard and Eco Saver?

Standard is normal delivery. Eco Saver may take a little longer.

Can I pay with Google Pay?

Yes. Choose a UPI app when you pay. If you'd like help with Google Pay, watch our Google Pay series on The Elders Club YouTube channel.

What should I check before I pay?

Always check your delivery address and your total. And never share your OTP or PIN with anyone who calls you.

What you'll walk away knowing

By the end of this guide, made free and step-by-step by The Elders Club, you'll be able to:

  • Find a restaurant and add food to your cart
  • Know what Swiggy One is, and why you can skip it
  • Choose a delivery type and add delivery instructions
  • Check your bill and pay, including Pay on delivery and UPI

Before you pay, always check your delivery address and your total. And never share your OTP or PIN with anyone who calls you.

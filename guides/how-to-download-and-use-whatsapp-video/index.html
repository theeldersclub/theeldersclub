Set up WhatsApp and make your first call

4:25 min
Written by Caroline D'Cruz, journalist and editor with 16+ years of experience, founder of The Elders Club · Last updated 13 September 2026

WhatsApp is how most families stay in touch today — but getting started can feel like a lot at once. This guide walks through creating your account, finding your way around the app, and making your first video or voice call, one screen at a time. None of it needs anyone else's phone or help once you've done it the first time — every step happens right there on your own screen.

The steps, written out

1

Agree to WhatsApp's terms

The first time you open WhatsApp, it asks you to accept its Terms of Service. Read the short summary if you'd like, then tap "Agree and continue" to move on.

On screen: Agree and continue screen

2

Confirm your number and verify with a code

WhatsApp sends a 6-digit code by SMS to confirm the number is really yours. Type the code in when it arrives — no need to copy or share it with anyone.

On screen: Entering the 6-digit code

3

Add your name and a profile picture

This is what your contacts will see when you message them. A profile picture is optional — you can skip it and add one later from Settings.

On screen: Edit profile screen

4

Know the four tabs at the bottom

Chats is every conversation you have. Communities groups several related chats together. Calls shows your voice and video call history. Updates has status posts and channels you follow.

On screen: Bottom navigation bar

5

Open a chat with anyone in seconds

Tap the search bar at the top of Chats and type a name — results appear instantly from your existing chats, groups, and phone contacts.

On screen: Search bar in Chats

6

Start a voice call

Open a chat and tap the phone icon at the top. You can add another person to the call, turn the speaker on or off, and mute your microphone whenever you need to.

On screen: Voice call screen

7

Start a video call

Open a chat and tap the video icon at the top — the icon right next to the phone icon. You can add another person, and turn your camera or the speaker on and off during the call.

On screen: Video call screen

8

Keep your verification code private

The 6-digit code WhatsApp sends you is yours alone. WhatsApp will never call or message you asking for it — anyone who does is trying to take over your account.

On screen: Reminder: never share your code

If something goes wrong

What if the 6-digit code doesn't arrive?

Wait a minute, then use the "Resend code" or "Call me" option that appears on screen — WhatsApp can read the code out to you over a phone call instead of texting it. Make sure you've typed your number correctly first.

Do I have to add a profile picture?

No, it's completely optional. Your contacts will still see your name. You can add or change a picture at any time later from the You tab.

What's the difference between a voice call and a video call?

A voice call is like a regular phone call — you only hear the other person. A video call also shows their face and yours, but uses more of your data or Wi-Fi. Both are free over an internet connection.

Is WhatsApp free to use?

Yes. Sending messages and making voice or video calls costs nothing beyond the mobile data or Wi-Fi you're already using — there are no separate WhatsApp charges.

Someone I don't know is calling me on WhatsApp — what should I do?

You don't have to answer. If you're unsure, let it go to nothing and check with family before calling back. Never share your 6-digit verification code with anyone who calls or messages asking for it, even if they claim to be from WhatsApp.

Can I use WhatsApp on more than one device?

Yes — WhatsApp can be linked to a computer or tablet at the same time as your phone, through Settings > Linked devices. Your phone still needs to stay connected to the internet for the others to work.

What you'll walk away knowing

By the end of this guide, made free and step-by-step by The Elders Club, you'll be able to:

  • Create your WhatsApp account
  • Find your way around the app
  • Open a chat with anyone in seconds
  • Make a video or voice call to family

Once you've made one video call this way, every call after it follows the same three taps — open the chat, tap the video icon, and wait for them to answer.

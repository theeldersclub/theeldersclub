Order groceries on Instamart

4:17 min
Written by Caroline D'Cruz, journalist and editor with 16+ years of experience, founder of The Elders Club · Last updated 14 October 2026

Instamart brings groceries and snacks to your door, and you can order from your own phone. This guide walks through downloading the app, signing in with your mobile number, finding items, adding them to your cart and paying, one screen at a time.

The steps, written out

1

Open the Play Store

On your phone, open the Play Store.

On screen: The Play Store icon

2

Search for Instamart

Tap the search bar at the top and type Instamart.

On screen: Typing Instamart in the search bar

3

Tap Install

Tap Install next to Instamart.

On screen: The Install button

4

Tap Open

When the button changes to Open, tap Open.

On screen: The Open button

5

Log in with your phone number

Tap Log in with phone number, then enter your mobile number.

On screen: The login screen

6

Type the code

You'll get a 6-digit code by SMS. Type it in.

On screen: The 6-digit code boxes

7

Allow location

Instamart will ask for your location. Tap While using the app.

On screen: The location pop-up

8

Allow order updates

To get order updates, tap Allow.

On screen: The notifications pop-up

9

See the home screen

This is Instamart's home screen. Your delivery address shows at the top.

On screen: Instamart home screen

10

Scroll to see categories

Scroll down to see the different categories, like Chips and Namkeen.

On screen: The category list

11

Choose a category and products

Tap a category, then scroll to see the products. Each one shows its price and size.

On screen: A list of products

12

Add what you want

Choose what you want and tap Confirm.

On screen: The Confirm button

13

Skip Swiggy One

Instamart may show a membership called Swiggy One. It's optional and paid, so tap Skip for now.

On screen: The Swiggy One pop-up

14

Check your cart

Check your items and the total.

On screen: The cart with the total

15

Tap Proceed to Pay

Tap Proceed to Pay at the bottom.

On screen: The Proceed to Pay button

16

Choose how to pay

Choose Pay on delivery to pay cash when the order arrives, or pay with a UPI app.

On screen: The list of payment options

If something goes wrong

Do I need Swiggy One to order on Instamart?

No. Swiggy One is an optional, paid membership with free delivery. The offer shown was Rs. 1 for 3 months, after which you may be charged. You can tap Skip for now and still order.

What does Pay on delivery mean?

It means you pay in cash when your groceries arrive, instead of paying in the app.

Can I pay with Google Pay?

Yes. Choose a UPI app when you pay. If you'd like help with Google Pay, watch our Google Pay series on The Elders Club YouTube channel.

Why does Instamart ask for my location?

So it can find items near you and deliver to the right place. Tap While using the app.

What should I check before I pay?

Always check your delivery address. And never share your OTP or PIN with anyone who calls you.

What you'll walk away knowing

By the end of this guide, made free and step-by-step by The Elders Club, you'll be able to:

  • Download Instamart and sign in
  • Find groceries and add them to your cart
  • Know what Swiggy One is, and why you can skip it
  • Pay, including Pay on delivery and UPI

Before you pay, always check your delivery address. And never share your OTP or PIN with anyone who calls you.
